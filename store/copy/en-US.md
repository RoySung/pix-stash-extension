## Name
PixStash

## Short description
Save web images and screenshots. Organize visual references with folders, tags, and notes.

## Detailed description
Keep the images and ideas you discover in a visual library of your own.

PixStash lets you save detected page images, capture the visible area of a tab, or select a region to capture. Gather design references and shopping candidates, then organize and review your choices.

• Save a detected page image or collect an image from its context menu.
• Capture the visible viewport or select a specific region.
• Organize references with folders, tags, and notes.
• Browse in grid or list view and find items by keyword, website, or tag.
• Keep source page information so you can revisit references.
• Use the interface in English or Traditional Chinese.

Your library is stored locally in this browser. Clearing extension data or uninstalling the extension may remove your library. Do not use it as your only backup.

Some protected browser pages and websites restrict capture or image access. Viewport capture does not create a full-page scrolling screenshot.
