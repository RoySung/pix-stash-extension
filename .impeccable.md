## Design Context

### Users
PixStash is for people collecting visual materials, references, and photos across the web.
They often use it while evaluating assets before purchase or while building a shortlist of candidates from multiple sites.
The core job is to collect first, compare later, and treat the extension like a cross-site materials cart for inspiration and selection.

### Brand Personality
The product should feel minimal, material-forward, and selection-oriented.
It should read as calm and curated rather than technical or productivity-heavy.
The emotional goal is to make collecting feel focused, trustworthy, and visually pleasant.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the approved restrained Windows XP Luna direction. A compact glossy blue title strip, beige tool surfaces, subtle beveled controls, yellow folder icons, and blue selection accents establish identity. Gallery surfaces stay white with charcoal system sans text and neutral gray metadata and tags.

### Design Principles
1. Put collected images first; retain complete-image previews and clear functional labels.
2. Use XP materials only on application chrome and controls. Do not add desktop wallpaper, taskbars, simulated window controls, address bars, or redundant menus.
3. Use bundled Noto Sans TC with Traditional Chinese system sans fallbacks. Body 14px, functional labels 13px, metadata at least 12px.
4. Restrict blue to title strips, links, focus, and selected controls. Navigation text remains charcoal; tags remain neutral.
5. Keep capture fast and expose organization after capture. Preserve current collection, tag, table, preview, and batch workflows.

### Geometry and States
- Panels use 6px corners and controls 3px corners. Shared controls have subtle bevels and 34px minimum height; icon controls retain clear hit targets.
- Use visible keyboard focus, distinct hover/pressed/disabled states, and existing reduced-motion support.
- Selected image cards have a blue border and visible checkbox without a blue title background or tinted image canvas.
- Floating batch controls appear only while selection exists. Shadows belong to transient surfaces.
- Library, Popup, Settings, dialogs, selects, and tag editors share tokens in `src/styles/theme.css`.
