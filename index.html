回報問題 / Report an issue

請提供瀏覽器與版本、PixStash 版本、重現步驟，以及不含個資的錯誤說明。請勿傳送密碼、登入權杖或敏感截圖。

Include your browser and extension versions, reproduction steps, and an error description without personal information. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or sensitive screenshots.

資料保存 / Your library

圖庫儲存在目前瀏覽器的本機空間。刪除資料夾不會刪除素材；清除擴充功能資料或移除擴充功能可能造成圖庫遺失。

Your library is stored locally in this browser. Deleting a folder does not delete its images. Clearing extension data or uninstalling may remove your library.
